A Neighborhood Gem

Located in the heart of Rock Hill, Elk Ave Tavern is your cozy, high-energy neighborhood spot. We pride ourselves on serving elevated comfort food paired with meticulously crafted cocktails in an atmosphere that feels like home. Whether you're here for a quick bite or a long evening with friends, our doors are open and the drinks are pouring.
